Weekly Meal Plan

Forza Raffaella! Every day is one step closer. Mario is with you. ♥

~1,500–1,650 kcal / day Gelato Sat & Sun Pizza once a week

Thursday

24 September1240 kcal
Breakfast
Fruit smoothie bowl 260 kcal
Ingredients 1 banana, 150g frozen berries, 100g Greek yogurt, 10g almonds
How Blend everything, pour in a bowl, top with sliced almonds.
Morning snack
Handful of almonds 100 kcal
Ingredients 10g almonds
How Eat as is.
Lunch
Tuna and bean salad 420 kcal
Ingredients 1 tin tuna, 150g cannellini beans, mixed salad, cherry tomatoes, 1 tsp olive oil
How Drain tuna and beans, mix with salad, dress with oil and lemon.
Afternoon snack
Clementine 60 kcal
Ingredients 1 clementine
How Peel and eat.
Dinner
Baked salmon with potatoes 400 kcal
Ingredients 120g salmon, 1 small potato, 150g green beans, 1 tsp olive oil, lemon
How Boil potato 20 min. Bake salmon and beans at 190C 15 min.

Friday

25 September1350 kcal
Breakfast
Scrambled eggs on toast 300 kcal
Ingredients 2 eggs, 1 slice whole wheat bread, half tomato, chives
How Scramble eggs on low heat 3 min. Serve on toast with tomato.
Morning snack
Apple 80 kcal
Ingredients 1 apple
How Eat fresh.
Lunch
Chicken soup with vegetables 430 kcal
Ingredients 100g chicken breast, 1 carrot, 1 onion, 1 celery stick, 50g small pasta, 1L chicken stock
How Boil chopped vegetables in stock 15 min. Add chicken and pasta, cook 10 min.
Afternoon snack
Greek yogurt with honey 120 kcal
Ingredients 100g Greek yogurt, 1 tsp honey
How Mix and eat.
Dinner
Turkey meatballs with tomato sauce 420 kcal
Ingredients 120g turkey mince, 150g passata, 30g breadcrumbs, basil, 1 tsp olive oil
How Form 5 meatballs, brown 5 min, add sauce, simmer 15 min.

Saturday

26 September1450 kcal
Breakfast
Banana oat pancakes with berries 320 kcal
Ingredients 1 banana, 2 eggs, 40g oats, 100g berries
How Blend banana, eggs and oats. Cook 3 pancakes 2 min per side. Top with berries.
Morning snack
Clementine 60 kcal
Ingredients 1 clementine
How Peel and eat.
Lunch
Quinoa bowl with feta 420 kcal
Ingredients 50g quinoa, 40g feta, half cucumber, cherry tomatoes, olives, 1 tsp olive oil
How Cook quinoa 15 min, chop vegetables, mix in a bowl.
Afternoon snack
Gelato reward 200 kcal
Ingredients 2 scoops of gelato or ice cream
How Enjoy - you earned it!
Dinner
Homemade thin-crust pizza night 450 kcal
Ingredients 1 whole wheat pizza base, 60g mozzarella, 100g passata, rocket, olives
How Spread passata, add mozzarella, bake at 220C 10 min. Top with rocket.

Sunday

27 September1430 kcal
Breakfast
Weekend croissant breakfast 320 kcal
Ingredients 1 croissant, 100g Greek yogurt, 1 orange
How Warm croissant. Serve with yogurt and fresh orange juice.
Morning snack
Apple 80 kcal
Ingredients 1 apple
How Eat fresh.
Lunch
Sunday roast-style chicken 480 kcal
Ingredients 120g chicken breast, 1 potato, 1 carrot, 100g green beans, 1 tsp olive oil
How Roast everything at 200C 35 min. Season with herbs and oil.
Afternoon snack
Gelato reward 200 kcal
Ingredients 2 scoops of gelato or ice cream
How Weekend treat - well done this week!
Dinner
Vegetable soup with whole wheat bread 350 kcal
Ingredients 1 courgette, 1 carrot, 1 potato, 1 tomato, 1L vegetable stock, 1 slice whole wheat bread
How Boil chopped vegetables in stock 25 min, blend half for creaminess. Serve with bread.
