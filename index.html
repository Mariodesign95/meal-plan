Weekly Meal Plan

Forza Raffaella! Every day is one step closer. Mario is with you. ♥

Around 1,600–1,800 kcal per day · postpartum-safe · rich in iron & calcium

Thursday, 24 September

Breakfast: Fruit smoothie bowl 240 kcal
Ingredients: 1 banana, 150g frozen berries, 100g Greek yogurt, 10g almonds
How: Blend everything. Pour into a bowl, top with sliced almonds.
Morning snack: Apple or pear 80 kcal
What: 1 apple or pear
Lunch: Tuna and bean salad 380 kcal
Ingredients: 1 tin tuna, 150g cannellini beans, mixed salad, cherry tomatoes, 1 tsp olive oil
How: Drain tuna and beans. Mix with salad and tomatoes, dress with oil and lemon.
Afternoon snack: Handful of almonds 100 kcal
What: 10g almonds or walnuts
Dinner: Whole wheat pasta with courgette 420 kcal
Ingredients: 70g whole wheat pasta, 1 courgette, 20g parmesan, 1 tsp olive oil, 1 garlic clove
How: Fry garlic and sliced courgette 8 min. Toss with drained pasta and parmesan.
Day total: ~1220 kcal

Friday, 25 September

Breakfast: Scrambled eggs on toast 280 kcal
Ingredients: 2 eggs, 1 slice whole wheat bread, half tomato, chives
How: Scramble eggs on low heat 3 min. Serve on toast with sliced tomato.
Morning snack: Handful of almonds 100 kcal
What: 10g almonds or walnuts
Lunch: Chicken soup with vegetables 380 kcal
Ingredients: 120g chicken breast, 1 carrot, 1 onion, 1 celery stick, 50g small pasta, 1L chicken stock
How: Chop vegetables, boil in stock 15 min. Add chicken and pasta, cook 10 more min.
Afternoon snack: Greek yogurt with honey 120 kcal
What: 100g Greek yogurt, 1 tsp honey
Dinner: Baked white fish with potatoes 360 kcal
Ingredients: 150g cod fillet, 1 potato, 100g green beans, 1 tsp olive oil, lemon
How: Boil potato 20 min. Bake fish with beans at 190C for 15 min. Season with oil and lemon.
Day total: ~1240 kcal

Saturday, 26 September

Breakfast: Banana oat pancakes with berries 300 kcal
Ingredients: 1 banana, 2 eggs, 40g oats, 100g berries, Greek yogurt
How: Blend banana, eggs and oats. Cook 3 pancakes 2 min per side. Top with berries and yogurt.
Morning snack: Greek yogurt with honey 120 kcal
What: 100g Greek yogurt, 1 tsp honey
Lunch: Quinoa bowl with feta 450 kcal
Ingredients: 60g quinoa, 50g feta, half cucumber, cherry tomatoes, olives, 1 tsp olive oil
How: Cook quinoa 15 min. Chop vegetables. Mix everything in a bowl.
Afternoon snack: Apple or pear 80 kcal
What: 1 apple or pear
Dinner: Turkey meatballs with tomato sauce 420 kcal
Ingredients: 150g turkey mince, 150g passata, 30g whole wheat breadcrumbs, basil
How: Mix mince with breadcrumbs, form 6 meatballs. Brown 5 min, add sauce, simmer 15 min.
Day total: ~1370 kcal

Sunday, 27 September

Breakfast: Weekend croissant breakfast 320 kcal
Ingredients: 1 croissant, 150g Greek yogurt, 1 orange
How: Warm croissant. Serve with yogurt and fresh orange juice.
Morning snack: Apple or pear 80 kcal
What: 1 apple or pear
Lunch: Roast-style chicken lunch 500 kcal
Ingredients: 120g chicken breast, 1 potato, 1 carrot, 100g green beans, 1 tsp olive oil
How: Roast everything at 200C for 35 min. Season with herbs and oil.
Afternoon snack: Handful of almonds 100 kcal
What: 10g almonds or walnuts
Dinner: Homemade thin-crust pizza night 600 kcal
Ingredients: 1 whole wheat pizza base, 80g mozzarella, 100g passata, rocket, olives
How: Spread passata on base, add mozzarella. Bake at 220C for 10 min. Top with rocket and olives.
Gelato reward: 2 scoops of gelato or ice cream 200 kcal
What: 2 scoops of your favourite gelato or ice cream
Day total: ~1800 kcal
