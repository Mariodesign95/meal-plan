🥗 Weekly Meal Plan

For healthy and sustainable weight loss

🎯 Goals

  • Daily calories: ~1600 kcal
  • Protein: 80-100g (maintain muscle)
  • Water: 2-3L per day
  • Note: Rich in iron & calcium, postpartum-safe

📅 Wednesday, 23 September

☀️ Breakfast: Greek yogurt with fruit 200 kcal
🥩 Prot 15g 🍞 Carbs 20g 🥑 Fat 5g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 150g Greek yogurt, 150g seasonal fruit, 10g walnuts

Recipe: Mix yogurt with fruit and nuts.
🍎 Morning snack: Fresh fruit 80 kcal
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 1 apple or pear

Recipe: Eat fresh.
🍽️ Lunch: Whole wheat pasta with tomato and basil 400 kcal
🥩 Prot 12g 🍞 Carbs 60g 🥑 Fat 8g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 70g whole wheat pasta, 150g tomatoes, basil, 1 tsp olive oil

Recipe: Cook pasta. Sauté tomatoes 5 min. Mix with pasta and oil.
🍎 Afternoon snack: Fresh fruit 80 kcal
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 1 apple or pear

Recipe: Eat fresh.
🌙 Dinner: Baked salmon with vegetables 400 kcal
🥩 Prot 35g 🍞 Carbs 15g 🥑 Fat 25g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 150g salmon, 200g zucchini, 1 small potato

Recipe: Bake salmon and vegetables for 20 min at 200°C.
Daily total: ~1600 kcal

📅 Thursday, 24 September

☀️ Breakfast: Whole wheat toast with avocado 250 kcal
🥩 Prot 8g 🍞 Carbs 25g 🥑 Fat 12g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 1 slice whole wheat bread, 1/2 avocado, 1 tsp olive oil

Recipe: Spread avocado on toast.
🍎 Morning snack: Nuts 100 kcal
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 10g almonds or walnuts

Recipe: Eat at room temperature.
🍽️ Lunch: Chicken and quinoa salad 450 kcal
🥩 Prot 35g 🍞 Carbs 45g 🥑 Fat 12g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 120g chicken breast, 50g quinoa, lettuce, cherry tomatoes, 1 tsp olive oil

Recipe: Cook quinoa. Grill chicken. Mix everything with dressing.
🍎 Afternoon snack: Nuts 100 kcal
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 10g almonds or walnuts

Recipe: Eat at room temperature.
🌙 Dinner: Grilled chicken with vegetables 350 kcal
🥩 Prot 40g 🍞 Carbs 10g 🥑 Fat 12g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 150g chicken breast, zucchini, aubergine, 1 tsp olive oil

Recipe: Grill chicken and vegetables. Drizzle with oil.
Daily total: ~1600 kcal

📅 Friday, 25 September

☀️ Breakfast: Oatmeal porridge 220 kcal
🥩 Prot 8g 🍞 Carbs 35g 🥑 Fat 4g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 30g oats, 200ml milk, half apple

Recipe: Cook oats with milk. Add diced apple.
🍎 Morning snack: Parmesan cheese 100 kcal
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 20g parmesan

Recipe: Eat with fruit.
🍽️ Lunch: Pumpkin risotto 420 kcal
🥩 Prot 10g 🍞 Carbs 70g 🥑 Fat 6g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 60g risotto rice, 200g pumpkin, 1/2 onion, 1 tsp olive oil

Recipe: Sauté pumpkin. Toast rice. Stir in pumpkin and oil.
🍎 Afternoon snack: Parmesan cheese 100 kcal
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 20g parmesan

Recipe: Eat with fruit.
🌙 Dinner: Spinach frittata 320 kcal
🥩 Prot 20g 🍞 Carbs 5g 🥑 Fat 25g
📄 Ingredients & Recipe
Ingredients: 2 eggs, 200g frozen spinach, grated cheese

Recipe: Sauté spinach. Add beaten eggs. Cook until golden.
Daily total: ~1600 kcal
